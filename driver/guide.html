1
Check Your Status

Note: Your employment type is set by Admin during registration. To switch from Freelance to Hired (or vice versa), please submit a formal request to HR/Admin Support. Unauthorized sharing of accounts between types is prohibited.
3. Calculating Fares & Logging Trips

Use this tool to verify fares before starting or after completing a trip. It syncs directly to your financial history.

Step A: Set Route
  1. Open Rate Calculator.
  2. Tap map to set Pickup (Green).
  3. Tap map again to set Drop-off (Red).
  4. The system calculates distance automatically.
Step B: Input & Save
  1. Enter Gas Price if changed today.
  2. Enter the Passenger Fare agreed upon.
  3. Click CALCULATE & SAVE TRIP.
  4. Review Net Income breakdown. Click OK to save to cloud.
Pro Tip: Always log every trip, even cash payments. This builds your performance score and helps Admins track fleet activity accurately. Unlogged trips do not count towards your Rank Points.
4. Tracking Expenses (Gas & Maintenance)

To know your true profit, you must deduct expenses from your gross income.

  1. Go to Expenses Log in the sidebar.
  2. Select Category: Fuel/Gas.
  3. Enter Amount Spent and Liters Purchased (optional but recommended for efficiency tracking).
  4. Attach Photo of Receipt (if available) for audit purposes.
  5. Click Save Expense.

  1. Go to Expenses Log.
  2. Select Category: Maintenance/Repair.
  3. Describe the service (e.g., "Oil Change", "Brake Pad").
  4. Enter Cost.
  5. Click Save Expense.
Why Track Expenses? The system uses this data to calculate your Net Profit per kilometer. High fuel efficiency = Higher potential earnings!
5. Withdrawing Your Earnings

Your earnings accumulate in your Digital Wallet. Follow these steps to cash out safely.

1
Check Available Balance

Go to My Wallet. Ensure your balance meets the Minimum Withdrawal Limit set by Admin (usually ₱500 or more).

2
Request Withdrawal

Click "Request Withdrawal". You will be asked to verify your identity using your login password and accept the Terms of Service.

3
Select Payment Method

Choose GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, or Cash Pickup. Enter the exact account number/name associated with that wallet.

4
Wait for Approval

Status changes to PENDING. Admin reviews requests daily. Once approved, status becomes PAID and you'll receive a Reference Number.

Important: Double-check your GCash/Maya number before submitting. Wrong numbers result in rejected transactions and delayed payouts.
6. Ranking System & Benefits

Your rank determines your priority in dispatching and commission rates. Earn points by completing verified trips.

Rank Points Required Commission Fee Benefits
Newbie 0 - 99 pts 20% Standard access
Regular 100 - 499 pts 18% Priority support
Senior 500 - 1499 pts 15% Higher visibility in Dispatch
Expert 1500 - 4999 pts 12% Exclusive bonuses & lower fees
Master 5000+ pts 10% VIP status, first choice of premium rides
Warning: Negative ratings from commuters or unresolved complaints will deduct points. Maintain high service quality to protect your rank.
7. Safety & Conduct
  • Wear helmet at all times while driving.
  • Provide spare helmets for passengers if available.
  • Do not accept rides under influence of alcohol/drugs.
  • Keep vehicle clean and well-maintained.
  • Respect passenger privacy and belongings.
  • Report any suspicious behavior to Admin immediately.
